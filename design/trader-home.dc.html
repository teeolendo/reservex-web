Why we built it

Investing back home shouldn’t take a broker, a branch and a cousin with power of attorney.

Kenya’s capital markets are deep, regulated and productive. Getting into them has been the hard part: paper forms, local bank accounts, wire fees and a lot of waiting. ReserveX Trader removes the friction, so you can put your money to work at home as easily as you spend it abroad.
